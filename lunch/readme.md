# Berkeley-Style Artichoke & Feta Pizza

## Description

A thin, crisp sourdough pizza piled with marinated artichoke hearts, red onion, mozzarella, and feta, finished with garlic oil, lemon zest, and fresh herbs. It's inspired by the one-veggie-pizza-a-day tradition at the Cheese Board Collective near UC Berkeley, where artichoke pies are a regular favorite.

### Ingredients:
- [ ] 1 lb sourdough or regular pizza dough (store-bought or homemade), at room temperature
- [ ] 1 (12 oz) jar marinated artichoke hearts, drained and roughly chopped
- [ ] 8 oz low-moisture mozzarella, shredded
- [ ] 2 oz feta, crumbled
- [ ] 1/2 small red onion, thinly sliced
- [ ] 3 garlic cloves, minced
- [ ] 3 tbsp extra-virgin olive oil, plus more for the pan
- [ ] 1 lemon, zested
- [ ] 2 tbsp fresh parsley, chopped
- [ ] 1/4 cup grated Parmesan
- [ ] 1/4 tsp red pepper flakes (optional)
- [ ] Salt and black pepper, to taste
- [ ] Cornmeal or flour for dusting

### Steps for preparation:

1. Place a pizza stone or an upside-down baking sheet on the middle rack and preheat the oven to 500°F (260°C) for at least 30 minutes.
2. Stir the minced garlic into the olive oil in a small bowl and set it aside to infuse.
3. Drain the artichoke hearts well, pat them dry with a paper towel, and chop them into bite-size pieces so they don't make the crust soggy.
4. Divide the dough into two equal pieces. On a floured surface, stretch or roll each piece into a 12-inch round about 1/4 inch thick.
5. Dust a pizza peel or a sheet of parchment with cornmeal and place one round of dough on it.
6. Brush the dough with half of the garlic oil, leaving the garlic bits on the surface but keeping a 1/2-inch border bare.
7. Sprinkle on half the mozzarella, then scatter half the artichokes, half the red onion, and half the feta evenly over the top.
8. Slide the pizza (or the parchment with the pizza on it) onto the hot stone and bake 12–15 minutes, until the crust is deep golden and the cheese is bubbling with browned spots.
9. Remove the pizza and immediately sprinkle with half the Parmesan, half the lemon zest, half the parsley, a pinch of red pepper flakes, and a little salt and pepper.
10. Let it rest 2 minutes, then slice and serve. Repeat steps 5–10 with the second pizza.

### Notes:

- **Herb swap:** Fresh basil or a little chopped green onion works in place of parsley.
- **Extra flavor:** Add kalamata olives or a few spoonfuls of pesto under the cheese.
- **Vegan option:** Skip the feta and Parmesan, use plant-based mozzarella, and finish with extra lemon and a drizzle of olive oil.
- **No pizza stone:** A preheated cast-iron skillet or an upside-down heavy baking sheet works fine.
- **Make-ahead:** Chop the toppings and make the garlic oil the night before. Store them covered in the fridge.
