# Chicken Tinga Taco Bar

## Description

Chicken tinga is a classic dish from Puebla, Mexico: shredded chicken simmered in a smoky tomato–chipotle sauce. Served taco-bar style with warm tortillas and toppings, it lets a whole group build their own plates.

### Ingredients:
- [ ] 2 lb boneless, skinless chicken thighs
- [ ] 4 cups water or low-sodium chicken broth
- [ ] 1 bay leaf
- [ ] 1 tsp salt, plus more to taste
- [ ] 2 tbsp vegetable oil
- [ ] 1 large white onion, thinly sliced
- [ ] 3 garlic cloves, minced
- [ ] 1 (14.5 oz) can fire-roasted diced tomatoes
- [ ] 2–3 chipotle peppers in adobo, plus 1 tbsp adobo sauce
- [ ] 1 tsp dried oregano (Mexican oregano if available)
- [ ] 1/2 tsp ground cumin
- [ ] 18 small corn tortillas
- [ ] 1 cup crumbled cotija or queso fresco
- [ ] 1/2 small red onion, finely diced
- [ ] 1 bunch cilantro, chopped
- [ ] 2 avocados, sliced (or 1 cup guacamole)
- [ ] 1 cup shredded cabbage or lettuce
- [ ] 1/2 cup Mexican crema or sour cream
- [ ] 3 limes, cut into wedges
- [ ] Salsa of choice, for serving

### Steps for preparation:

1. Put the chicken thighs, water or broth, bay leaf, and 1 tsp salt in a pot. Bring to a boil, then lower the heat and simmer, covered, for 20 minutes until the chicken is cooked through.
2. Transfer the chicken to a cutting board, reserving 1 cup of the cooking liquid. Let the chicken cool slightly, then shred it with two forks.
3. Blend the canned tomatoes, chipotle peppers, adobo sauce, oregano, cumin, and the reserved 1 cup cooking liquid until smooth.
4. Heat the oil in a large skillet over medium heat. Add the sliced onion and cook 5–7 minutes, stirring, until soft and lightly golden.
5. Add the garlic and cook 1 minute, until fragrant.
6. Pour in the blended sauce and simmer 5 minutes, until slightly thickened.
7. Stir in the shredded chicken and simmer 5 more minutes so it absorbs the sauce. Taste and add salt if needed.
8. Warm the tortillas one at a time in a dry skillet over medium-high heat, about 30 seconds per side, then stack them in a clean kitchen towel to keep warm.
9. Set out the chicken, tortillas, and all toppings (cheese, red onion, cilantro, avocado, cabbage, crema, lime wedges, and salsa) in separate bowls so everyone can build their own tacos.

### Notes:

- **Heat level:** Use 1 chipotle for mild, 3 or more for spicy.
- **Vegetarian option:** Swap the chicken for two cans of drained black beans or shredded jackfruit, and use vegetable broth.
- **Make-ahead:** The tinga keeps 3 days in the fridge and tastes better the next day. Reheat gently with a splash of water.
- **Bigger crowd:** The recipe doubles easily. Keep the chicken warm in a slow cooker on the "warm" setting.
